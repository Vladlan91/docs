**Market** is the section of the Revol Wallet app where customers search for companies by name, category, city or "nearby" and get a discount from Revol without any QR code or promo code. To be listed, a company needs two things: the **Public** status and a signed **partnership with Revol**.

## Partnership terms

The terms are the same for every company and cannot be edited.

| Parameter | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Your pool for Revol | **5%** of the offer amount | What you give away for a customer brought by the Market |
| Customer discount | **2%** | The part of the pool the customer receives as a discount at your checkout |
| Revol commission | **3%** | The part of the pool accrued to Revol as the source |

Commission is accrued only from **paid** orders where the customer's right was applied. The offer amount, the base for both discount and commission, is calculated exactly as for regular partnerships (see [Promo codes on orders](/en/referral/promo-codes)). Everything else works like a regular partnership: journal, settlements, analytics, terms history.

<Note>
  If you already have a direct partnership with the Revol company on different terms, it stays as it is. To move to the fixed terms, end the direct partnership first, then sign the Revol partnership from the card below.
</Note>

## How to get listed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the company profile">
    Customers in the Market see what is filled in under **Company settings**: name and logo, description, categories, phone, website, social networks, available services with prices, gallery and videos. An empty profile is shown, but it does not convince anyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your locations">
    In Company settings open the **Locations** card and choose where you operate: the whole country, regions or cities. Several are allowed; the first one is primary. The "City" and "Nearby" filters rely on locations: a company without them does not appear in those filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the company public">
    In Company settings change the status to **Public**. Or press **Make public** right in the "Partnership with Revol" card on **My referrals → Partners**. Statuses: *Draft* — work in progress; *Active* — the profile is visible to nobody; *Public* — visible in the Market once the Revol partnership is signed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the partnership">
    In the "Partnership with Revol" card press **Sign partnership** and confirm the terms. No token, number verification or terms negotiation is needed: the partnership becomes active immediately and promo codes are issued in the same step. The card shows the **In Market** badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The company owner or a member with the `referral` platform can sign the partnership and change the status.

## What the customer sees

1. Opens the **Market** tab in the app, searches by name, picks a category, a city or "Nearby".
2. Opens the company profile: description, contacts, addresses, services, gallery, videos and the "Discount from Revol" offer.
3. Taps **Get discount** — the right appears in the wallet. It is the same kind of right as the one received by scanning a group QR (see [Groups and the app](/en/referral/groups-and-wallet)): validity period, one use, one transfer to a friend, a card at checkout.
4. At your checkout shows the card; the manager scans it with the order scanner, the discount is applied, and after payment the Revol commission is accrued.

If the discount was already claimed, the profile shows **In wallet**; if the customer transferred it to a friend, it is not issued again.

## Visibility in the Market

| Company status | Revol partnership | In the Market |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Active | **Yes** |
| Public | None or ended | No |
| Active or Draft | Active | No: the partnership works, but the company is hidden |

- Changing the status from Public to anything else **does not end the partnership**: the company disappears from the Market, already issued discounts keep working. The system warns you before the change.
- Ending the Revol partnership works like a regular one: no new discounts are issued, issued ones keep working for the grace period, accrued commissions and balances do not change.
- Pausing the partnership stops issuing new discounts but does not hide the company from the Market while it is public.

## Common errors

| Message | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Make the company public first | Status is not Public | Change the status in settings or with the button in the card |
| A Revol partnership already exists | The partnership is already signed | Open it from the card |
| Revol partnership terms are fixed and cannot be edited | An attempt to change pool, discount or commission | Terms do not change; end the partnership if needed |
| The company does not appear in the "City" or "Nearby" filter | No locations set | Add locations in settings |
| Discount is currently unavailable (in the app) | The partnership is paused or ended, or codes are inactive | Check the partnership status on the Partners page |
